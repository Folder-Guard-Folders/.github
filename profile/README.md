## First Look at Folder Guard

Folder Guard is for people who keep private folders they own and want one named guard desk, not unlocked paths on the same PC. It gives you private folders, an own PC, and a short path back to a locked path you already keep. It does not replace the rest of your machine. It sits beside the folders you already use.

Work stays on folders you own. Extra paths wait for their own title.
